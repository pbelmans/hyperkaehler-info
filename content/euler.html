Euler characteristic

The topological Euler characteristic $\mathrm{e}(X)$ is the alternating sum of the Betti numbers of a manifold. It is also the top Chern number, so for a $2n$-dimensional hyperkähler manifold $X$ we are interested in $\int\mathrm{c}_{2n}(X)$.

K3[n]-type
The Euler characteristics are the coefficients of the expansion of $1/\eta(q)^{24}$, see also OEIS:A006922. This is proven in {{< cite "MR1032930" "Corollary 2.10(b)" >}}. Written out (see Theorem 0.1 of op. cit) it reads \[ \sum_{n=0}\mathrm{e}(\mathrm{K3}^{[n]})t^n=\prod_{m=1}^{+\infty}(1-t^m)^{-24} \]
Kumn-type
The Euler characteristics are the coefficients of the expansion of $\frac{1}{6912}(6\mathrm{E}_2^2\mathrm{E}_4 - 8\mathrm{E}_2\mathrm{E}_6 + 3\mathrm{E}_4^2 - \mathrm{E}_2^4)$, where $\mathrm{E}_2,\mathrm{E}_4,\mathrm{E}_6$ are the Eisenstein series of weights 2, 4, and 6, see also OEIS:A282211. In a closed formula (see {{< cite "MR1219901" "Corollary 1" >}}) it reads \[ \mathrm{e}(\mathrm{Kum}^{n})=(n+1)^3\sum_{d\mathrel{|} n+1}d \]
OG6
In {{< cite "MR2282256" "Theorem 2.2.3" >}} it is shown that \[ \mathrm{e}(\mathrm{OG}_6)=1920 \]
OG10
In {{< cite "mozgovoy-phd" >}} it is shown that \[ \mathrm{e}(\mathrm{OG}_{10})=176904 \]
