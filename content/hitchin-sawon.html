Norm of the Riemann curvature tensor

Hitchin and Sawon computed in {{< cite "MR1813238" >}} (see also Sawon's thesis {{< cite "sawon-phd" >}}) the $\mathcal{L}^2$ norm of the Riemann curvature tensor $R$ of a hyperkähler manifold $X$ of (real) dimension $4k$: \[ \frac{1}{(192\pi^2k)^k}\frac{||R||^{2k}}{(\operatorname{vol} X)^{k-1}}=\int_X\mathrm{td}_X^{1/2} \]
